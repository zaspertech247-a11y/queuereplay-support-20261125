Privacy

Workloads and reports

QueueReplay stores the workload titles and job details you enter, saved scenarios, and frozen reports in the library on your device. The calculation runs on your device. The app does not require registration or use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Sharing and deletion

When you choose Share PDF or Share CSV, the system share sheet lets you select a destination. You can delete individual saved items, or use About → Clear local library to remove the saved library from QueueReplay. Copies sent or saved through a share destination are managed there.
