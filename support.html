Try the example

  1. On Workloads, tap Load sample, then Open replay.
  2. At 6 seconds the station is serving B while C and D wait. Move the slider, tap or drag the chart, or use Event ledger to jump to an event.
  3. Tap Compare presets to see an alternate rule, or use Station count in Compare to change the number of stations.
  4. Tap Freeze report or Freeze comparison. In Report Detail use Share PDF or Share CSV. The Reports tab stores the frozen result.

Enter your own jobs

Tap New workload, enter a name, and use Add job and Apply job for each entry. Tap Save workload. Use whole seconds or minutes, up to 250 jobs, and one to three identical stations.
